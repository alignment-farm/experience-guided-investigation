## The next bounded contribution

Develop the smallest comparison that resolves these gaps. These are evidential
milestones, not a prescribed editor, training recipe or full factorial protocol.

1. **Establish usable actions and complete development behavior.** Find a
   model/interface combination that can inspect, edit, obtain informative execution
   feedback and finish a repair correctly. Validate the actual stated contract,
   including relevant preserved behavior. Improve shared tools and feedback in
   both arms; separate that improvement from an effect of learned experience.
   Diagnose acquisition purposefully before treating a transfer failure as a
   learning limit. Finite gradients, decreasing loss and recognizable action
   sequences do not establish useful acquisition.
2. **Acquire from eligible earlier work with a competent reference.** Collect
   actual participant attempts and feedback. Derive teaching or corrections from
   those attempts and identify teacher interventions. Preserve the same eligible
   full records, code, tests, ordinary notes and reusable answers for ordinary
   continuation. Make any context-delivery difference explicit. Establish whether
   earlier experience helps formulate and use a distinguishing check on new work,
   rather than merely selecting a tool name or replaying a known answer. An
   informative matched-workspace comparison can separate additional learned state
   from improvements already retained in code and tests.
3. **Freeze the developed claim and evaluate fresh work.** Hold out subsequent
   requirements and confirmation evidence while developing the treatment. Reset
   conversations and ephemeral state; inventory what persists and verify loading.
   Evaluate complete new and still-valid behavior together. Compare against
   ordinary continuation and, when there is useful behavior to price, a meaningful
   alternative allocation to current-task inspection/testing/repair. Allow unused
   budget and retain competent caching and source reuse. Include failed acquisition,
   teacher work, training, retrieval, inference, testing and repair in native costs.
