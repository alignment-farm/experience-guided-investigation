No adapter was saved or used. Preserve this failure. The cap is not treated as a reliable hard ceiling. Use a fresh base-initialized attempt with the SAME 23 targets, two epochs/46 updates, rank/optimizer/seed. This preserves conditioning on every prefix token but deliberately omits gradients through prefix K/V construction; it is NOT equivalent to full-context SFT gradients. No context truncation or persistent cross-example cache. Verify forward-loss agreement with ordinary full-context forward at small and full-row sizes before updates.
